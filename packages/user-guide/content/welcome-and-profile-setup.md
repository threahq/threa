# Your welcome setup

The first time you join a workspace, Threa shows a page titled **Welcome**, with the line "Complete your profile to get started". It sets the name and handle other people see in that workspace. You get to it when you accept an invitation, for example by selecting **Accept** on a pending invitation in the workspace picker.

## The Welcome page

The page has four fields:

- **Display name**: how others see you in this workspace.
- **Display slug**: the handle used for @mentions. Leave it blank to have one generated from your name.
- **Timezone**: starts as your browser's timezone.
- **Locale**: starts as your browser's locale.

The slug follows your name as you type until you edit it yourself. Threa checks a short moment after you stop typing whether the slug is free. If someone already has it, the page shows "This slug is already taken in this workspace." and you can't continue until you change it.

Select **Complete Setup** to finish. You land on the workspace home.

## Edit your profile later

Open the user menu in the sidebar and select **Settings**, which opens the **Profile** tab. It has:

- **Change photo**, plus **Remove** once you have one. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 50 MB.
- **Status**, with a **Set a status** button.
- **Display name**, up to 100 characters. A **Save** button appears once you change it.
- **Email**, which is read-only. Your email is your identity in the workspace, so it changes only when you change your account email.
- **Pronouns**: None, he/him, she/her, they/them, xe/xem, or Custom.
- **Description**, up to 500 characters.
- **Phone** and **GitHub**.

The display slug isn't editable on this tab.

The sidebar's **Getting started** checklist has an **Add a profile photo** item that opens this tab. See [A tour of the sidebar](/guide/sidebar-tour). Settings for appearance, notifications and the rest are covered in [Settings overview](/guide/settings-overview).

# Finding your settings

Your personal settings open in a dialog titled **Settings**. Settings for the workspace as a whole are in a separate dialog, **Workspace Settings**.

## Open Settings

- Press Ctrl+. (⌘. on Mac).
- Open the menu in the sidebar footer and choose **Settings**.
- Open the command palette (Ctrl+Shift+K, ⌘⇧K on Mac) and choose **Open Settings**, or **Settings:** followed by a tab name to go straight to a tab.

## The tabs

- **Profile**: identity and account details. See [Your profile](/guide/profile-settings).
- **AI**: scratchpad guidance and behavior. See [AI settings](/guide/ai-settings).
- **Dictation**: voice model, polish and steering words. See [Voice dictation](/guide/voice-dictation).
- **Calls**: self-view mirror and default layout. See [Call layouts and devices](/guide/call-layouts-and-devices).
- **Appearance**: theme and message density. See [Appearance and accessibility](/guide/appearance-and-accessibility).
- **Date & Time**: timezone and formatting. See [Dates, time zones and working hours](/guide/date-time-and-working-hours).
- **Working hours**: working week and shifts, on the same article.
- **Notifications**: alerts and push behavior. See [Notification levels per stream](/guide/notification-levels) and [Push notifications](/guide/push-notifications).
- **Composer**: sending, attachments and shortcuts. See [Composer and keyboard settings](/guide/composer-settings).
- **Accessibility**: motion, contrast, fonts and composer layout. See [Appearance and accessibility](/guide/appearance-and-accessibility).
- **Privacy**: crash reports and usage data, session recording, and whether agents are told which device you're on. See [Privacy and diagnostics options](/guide/privacy-settings).
- **Diagnostics**: performance data sharing. It only appears where performance diagnostics have been turned on for you or your workspace.

## Elsewhere in the app

The menu in the sidebar footer also holds:

- **Pause notifications** (see [Pausing notifications and setting a status](/guide/pause-and-status)).
- **Getting started** and **Customize sidebar** (see [Customize your sidebar](/guide/customize-your-sidebar)).
- Theme choices.
- **Workspace Settings**, for the workspace as a whole (see [Workspace general settings](/guide/workspace-general-settings)).
- **AI Usage** (see [AI usage and budget](/guide/ai-usage-and-budget)).
- **App status** (see [Updates, offline and app status](/guide/updates-offline-and-app-status)). The page is also at [App status](app:app-status).
- **Switch account** and **Log out**.

Your name card in the same menu opens the status picker.

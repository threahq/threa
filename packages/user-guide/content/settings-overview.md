# Finding your settings

All settings open in one window titled **Settings**. It opens on **Overview**, which lists every page with its current values. The **Search settings** box finds a setting by name or by what it does.

## Open Settings

- Press Ctrl+. (⌘. on Mac).
- Open the menu in the sidebar footer and choose **Settings**. **Profile** in the same menu opens the Profile page.
- Open the command palette (Ctrl+Shift+K, ⌘⇧K on Mac) and choose **Open settings**, or **Settings:** followed by a page name to go straight to a page.

## The pages

The pages are grouped under **You** and **Workspace**. When a stream is open, a **This stream** group with that stream's pages comes first (see [Stream settings](/guide/stream-settings)).

Under **You**:

- **Profile**: photo, name, status, pronouns and contact details. See [Your profile](/guide/profile-settings).
- **Notifications**: push, pause, delivery and quiet hours. See [Notification levels per stream](/guide/notification-levels) and [Push notifications](/guide/push-notifications).
- **Appearance**: theme, text size, font, motion and contrast. See [Appearance and accessibility](/guide/appearance-and-accessibility).
- **Reading**: unreads, link previews and long content.
- **Sidebar & board**: Inbox clearing, labels and the board.
- **Keyboard & composer**: the send key, send button and shortcuts. See [Composer and keyboard settings](/guide/composer-settings).
- **Time & schedule**: time zone, clock, date format and working hours. See [Dates, time zones and working hours](/guide/date-time-and-working-hours).
- **Your AI**: your default agent for new scratchpads, your agents and your instructions. See [AI settings](/guide/ai-settings).
- **Voice & calls**: dictation, your dictation words, and call video and layout. See [Voice dictation](/guide/voice-dictation) and [Call layouts and devices](/guide/call-layouts-and-devices).
- **Privacy & security**: encrypted scratchpads, usage data and device sharing. See [Privacy and diagnostics options](/guide/privacy-settings).
- **API keys & bots**: your personal API keys and personal bots. See [Personal API keys](/guide/personal-api-keys) and [Bots](/guide/bots-overview).
- **Early access**: features turned on for you early. It appears only if your account has features switched away from their defaults.

Under **Workspace**:

- **Members**: people, invitations and guest direct messages. See [Members, invitations and roles](/guide/members-invitations-and-roles).
- **Workspace details**: name, data region and AI spend time zone. See [Workspace general settings](/guide/workspace-general-settings).
- **Defaults & presets**: default working hours, status presets and workspace dictation words. See [Statuses and default working hours](/guide/workspace-statuses-and-schedule).
- **Workspace AI**: workspace agents, models, sandbox and spend limits. Only workspace admins see it. See [Managing AI agents](/guide/workspace-ai-agents).
- **Integrations**: GitHub and Linear. See [GitHub and Linear](/guide/github-and-linear).
- **Shared bots**: bots everyone in the workspace can use. See [Bots](/guide/bots-overview).

Workspace pages you can't change are marked **View only**.

## Elsewhere in the app

The menu in the sidebar footer also holds:

- **Pause notifications** (see [Pausing notifications and setting a status](/guide/pause-and-status)).
- **Getting started** and **Customize sidebar** (see [Customize your sidebar](/guide/customize-your-sidebar)).
- Theme choices.
- **Profile** and **Settings**, which open the settings window.
- **AI usage** (see [AI usage and budget](/guide/ai-usage-and-budget)).
- **App status** (see [Updates, offline and app status](/guide/updates-offline-and-app-status)). The page is also at [App status](app:app-status).
- **Switch account** and **Log out**.

Your name card in the same menu opens the status picker.

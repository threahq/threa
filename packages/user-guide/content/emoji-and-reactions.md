# Emoji and reactions

You can react to any message with an emoji. Reactions appear as small pills under the message, each with a count. Threa uses the standard Unicode emoji set. There is no setting for adding your own emoji to a workspace.

## Reacting

- On a desktop browser, hover the message and use the emoji button in the toolbar that floats above it. Its label is **Add reaction**.
- On a phone or tablet, long-press the message. The action sheet opens with a row of quick reactions at the top. Tap one to react, or tap **More reactions** to open the full picker.
- Click or tap a pill under the message to add your own reaction of the same emoji. Click it again to remove yours. A pill with a highlighted border is one you have used. The small **Add reaction** button at the end of the row opens the picker.

## The picker

The picker has a **Search emoji...** box. Without a search, it shows a **Reactions** section with the emoji already used on this message, a **Recently used** section, and then the full list. Recently used is ordered by how often you have used each emoji lately, in messages and in reactions.

Use the arrow keys to move through the grid, Enter to pick, and Escape to close.

## Who reacted

Hover a reaction pill to see who used it. The card has an **All** tab with the total count and one tab per emoji, and each row names a person and the emoji they used. On a touch screen, long-press a pill for the same list. Tapping a pill still toggles your reaction. The row shows the five most-used emoji. When a message has more than five different emoji, a **+N** button opens the rest.

## Emoji in the composer

To put an emoji in the text of a message, type **:** and a few letters, or use the **Emoji** button in the composer. See [Mentions and channel links](/guide/mentions-and-links).

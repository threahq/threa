# Statuses and default working hours

Two tabs in Workspace Settings set defaults for everyone: **Statuses** and **Working hours**. Only workspace admins can change them. Other members can open both tabs and see the current values, with a note that only workspace admins can make changes.

## Statuses

The **Statuses** tab lists the status presets members can pick from. Each preset has:

- An emoji, picked with the emoji button.
- **Status text**, up to 100 characters. A preset needs an emoji, text, or both.
- A default duration: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, Until tomorrow, Until next workday, This week, or Don't clear.
- A bell toggle, **Pause notifications for this status**. While a status with the bell on is active, notifications are paused.

A workspace starts with six system presets: Focus mode, Out and about, Out of office, Sick, Do not disturb and VAB. Do not disturb is the one that pauses notifications.

Admins can add presets with **Add status** (up to 25), edit or remove existing ones, and save with **Save statuses**. **Use system defaults** puts the six system presets back in the list; it still needs **Save statuses** to take effect. Saving replaces the whole list, so a customised list is the only list members see from the workspace.

Members can also save their own presets, which appear in addition to the workspace's. Setting a status is covered in [Pause and status](/guide/pause-and-status).

## Working hours

The **Working hours** tab sets the **Default working schedule**: the working week and hours that members inherit unless they set their own. Scheduling shortcuts such as "Tomorrow morning" and "Next week" snap to the start of work.

For each weekday you can switch the day on or off. A day that is off shows **Day off**. A working day has one or more shifts with a start and end time, and **Add shift** adds another, for example an afternoon block after a morning one. A day can have up to six shifts. A new workspace uses Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.

Edit the schedule, then choose **Save schedule**. **Reset** discards your unsaved edits.

Members can override the default under **Working hours** in their own settings by turning on a custom schedule. See [Date, time and working hours](/guide/date-time-and-working-hours).

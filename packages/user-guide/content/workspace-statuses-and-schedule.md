# Statuses and default working hours

The [Defaults & presets](app:settings/workspace-defaults) page of the settings window sets defaults for everyone: **Working hours**, **Statuses** and **Dictation words**. Only workspace admins can change them. Other members can open the page and see the current values, with the note "Only workspace admins can change this."

## Statuses

**Status presets** lists the statuses members can pick from, next to their own. Each preset has:

- An emoji, picked with the emoji button, and a **Name** of up to 100 characters. A preset needs an emoji, a name, or both.
- **Clears after**: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, Until tomorrow, Until next workday, This week, or Don't clear.
- **Pauses notifications**: while a status with this checked is active, no notifications reach whoever has it set.

A workspace starts with six system presets: Focus mode, Out and about, Out of office, Sick, Do not disturb and VAB. Do not disturb is the one that pauses notifications.

Admins add a preset with **New status** (up to 25), change one with **Edit**, and remove one with its remove button. Each change saves right away. Once the list differs from the system presets, **Reset to defaults** puts the six system presets back for everyone, after a confirmation.

Members can also save their own presets, which appear in addition to the workspace's. Setting a status is covered in [Pause and status](/guide/pause-and-status).

## Working hours

**Default working hours** are the working week and hours that members get until they set their own. Scheduling shortcuts such as "Tomorrow morning" and "Next week" snap to the start of work. Open the row to edit the schedule.

Each weekday has a switch to turn it on or off. A day that is off shows **Day off**. A working day has one or more shifts with a start and end time, and **Add shift** adds another, for example an afternoon block after a morning one. A day can have up to six shifts. A new workspace uses Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00. Each edit saves right away.

Members can set their own hours under **Your working hours** on the **Time & schedule** page by choosing **Customize**. See [Date, time and working hours](/guide/date-time-and-working-hours).

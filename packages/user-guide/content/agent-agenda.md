# The Agent agenda

The [Agent agenda](app:agenda) lists the follow-ups your agents scheduled, the tasks they delegated and the subagents they started. Use it to see what is waiting, and to cancel or finish items.

## Open it

- Click **Agent agenda** in the sidebar. You can show or hide this quick link when you customize the sidebar (see [Customize your sidebar](/guide/customize-your-sidebar)).
- Press Ctrl+Shift+O (⌘⇧O on Mac). If you are in a stream, it opens limited to that stream.
- Use **Agent agenda in this stream** in the quick switcher, or **Agent agenda…** in a stream's menu.

When opened for a stream, a scope chip limits the list to that stream. Remove it with the X on the chip.

## Find and filter

- The search box, **Search follow-ups and delegated tasks**, filters the list.
- **Outstanding** (the default), **Settled** and **All** filter by state.
- **Follow-ups**, **Delegations** and **Subagents** filter by kind. Click a kind again to clear it.

Rows show an icon for the kind, the title, a status, the stream and the time. They are grouped as **Now** (outstanding work whose time has passed), then by day, then **Later** (more than 6 days ahead).

## Statuses

- Follow-ups: Scheduled, Ran, Cancelled, Failed.
- Delegations: Open, Claimed, Running, Completed, Failed, Cancelled, Claim expired.
- Subagents: Working, Waiting for you, Done, Failed, Cancelled, Expired.

## Details and actions

Select a row to see its kind, stream, scheduled time, when it was created, the last change, who claimed it and its note. On a narrow screen the detail replaces the list, and **Back to the agenda list** returns to it. On a wide screen the up and down arrow keys move the selection.

- **Open in stream** goes to the item's stream.
- **Cancel** cancels a follow-up or an unfinished delegation.
- **Mark done** finishes an unfinished delegation.
- **Requeue** reopens a delegation whose claim expired.

A subagent is cancelled from its card in the conversation.

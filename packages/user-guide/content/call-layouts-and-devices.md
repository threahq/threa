# Call layouts and devices

A call opens in a surface that stays visible as you move around the workspace. You can change the surface, the layout of the tiles, and the camera, microphone and speaker you use.

## Where the call appears on desktop

Use the button with the title **Change call view** in the call's header to pick a surface:

- **Floating**: a square you can drag anywhere. Minimize it to a small bar with the controls, and use **Restore call** to bring it back.
- **Sidebar**: docked down the right side, and you can resize it by dragging its left edge. Minimizing it leaves a narrow strip with the timer, avatars, mute, camera and leave. Hovering the strip shows the call, and **Keep call open** pins it open.
- **Fullscreen**: fills the area beside the navigation.

Which surface a call opens in is the **Desktop video** setting: **Keep last**, **Floating square**, **Sidebar** or **Fullscreen**.

## Where the call appears on mobile

On a phone the call is a drawer at the top of the screen. Drag its handle to move between four sizes, from a small pill up to full screen. It stays in place when you navigate to another stream. In the full-screen size, **Collapse call** shrinks it again.

## Speaker and grid

Fullscreen on desktop and the full-screen drawer on mobile have a **Call layout** switch:

- **Speaker**: one large tile, a small view of yourself you can drag, and the other participants as thumbnails.
- **Grid**: equal tiles for everyone, in two columns up to four people and three beyond that.

The large tile in Speaker layout is not picked by who is talking. It is the first other person in the call until you select a thumbnail to pin someone. On desktop, a second button changes **thumbnail placement** between below and beside the video.

The **Default layout** setting decides which layout a call opens with. You can still switch during the call.

## Call controls

The controls are **Mute**, the camera toggle, **Flip camera** (mobile only, when the device has more than one camera), **Devices**, **Connection diagnostics**, **Open call chat** and **Leave call**.

**Devices** opens a menu with:

- **Mirror my video**: **Automatic**, **On** or **Off**. Only your own view is mirrored. Everyone else sees you the normal way.
- **Camera**: on desktop only. On mobile, use **Flip camera**.
- **Microphone**
- **Speaker**: in browsers that let a page choose an output device.

**Connection diagnostics** (the signal icon) shows round-trip time, packet loss and a quality reading (None, Limited by bandwidth, Limited by CPU or Limited) for the call. It also has a lifecycle log you can copy, which helps when reporting a problem.

## Call settings

Open [Settings](app:settings/calls) and choose **Calls**:

- **Mirror my video**: **Automatic** mirrors a front-facing or desktop camera and leaves a phone's back camera normal. **Always mirror** and **Never mirror** override that.
- **Default layout**: **Speaker** or **Grid**.
- **Desktop video**: where a call opens on desktop.

To start or join a call, see [Starting and joining calls](/guide/calls-basics).

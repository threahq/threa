# Pausing notifications and setting a status

You can silence push notifications on all your devices for a while, either directly or through a status.

## Pause notifications

Open the menu in the sidebar footer and choose **Pause notifications**, or use the **Pause notifications** section of your [notification settings](app:settings/notifications). The dialog silences notifications on all your devices for a while, independent of your status. Choose one of:

- **For 30 minutes**, **For 1 hour** or **For 2 hours**.
- **Until tomorrow**.
- **Until I turn it back on**.
- **Custom duration…**, then **Pause**.
- **Until a specific time…**. A time in the past gives the message **Pick a time in the future**.

While paused, the menu shows **Resume notifications**, and an indicator reads **Notifications paused** or **Notifications paused until …**. Pausing holds back push only. Your [activity feed](/guide/activity-and-unread) still records activity. An incoming call sends no push either, and its ring sound stays off, though the call card still shows. A manual pause overrides your notification level while it lasts, and it stays in place when you change your status.

## Set a status

Click your name card in the sidebar footer, or use **Status** in your [profile settings](app:settings/profile), to open **Set a status**. Pick an emoji, type what your status is (up to 100 characters, prompt **What's your status?**) or choose a preset, then click **Set status**.

**Clear after** sets when the status ends: **Don't clear**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **4 hours**, **Until tomorrow**, **Until next workday**, **This week**, **Custom duration…** or **Custom time…**. Click **Clear status** to remove it early. **Save as preset** keeps your own text for reuse, up to 25 presets.

The built-in presets are:

- **Focus mode**, 60 minutes.
- **Out and about**, 4 hours.
- **Out of office**, until tomorrow.
- **Sick**, until the next working day.
- **Do not disturb**, with no end, and pausing notifications.
- **VAB**, with no end.

Workspace admins can replace these with their own (see [Workspace statuses and schedule](/guide/workspace-statuses-and-schedule)).

## Statuses that pause notifications

The **Pause notifications** switch in the status dialog ("Don't notify me while this status is set") makes the status pause push for as long as it lasts. When the status expires or is cleared, so does that pause.

A pause that comes from a status can't be resumed from the menu. Notification settings say **Set by your status** and tell you to clear the status to resume.

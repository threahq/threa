# A tour of the sidebar

The sidebar lists your streams and the standing views of your workspace. Its layout is yours to change, see [Customizing the sidebar](/guide/customize-your-sidebar). This article describes the default layout, which is called Smart.

## Header

- The workspace name links back to the workspace picker.
- **History** shows Back and Forward and your recently visited streams.
- The search icon, labelled "Search messages", opens [Search](app:search). See [Search basics](/guide/search-basics).
- The command icon, labelled "Jump to stream or command", opens a menu with **Jump to stream**, **Commands** and **Browse Streams**. The first two open the [quick switcher](/guide/quick-switcher-and-command-palette).

## Quick Links

The **Quick Links** block links to the standing views:

- [Drafts](app:drafts), [Saved](app:saved) and [Scheduled](app:scheduled), with counts.
- [Streams](app:streams), [Files](app:files), Agent agenda, [Memory](app:memory) and [Labels](app:labels).
- [Activity](app:activity), with an unread badge.

Each link can be shown, hidden, or (for Drafts, Saved, Scheduled and Activity) shown only when it has something to show. Hidden links sit behind a "more" fold.

## Stream sections

Under Quick Links, your streams are grouped automatically:

- **Important**: streams where someone mentioned you, or where an AI persona wrote something you haven't read. It shows at most 10.
- **Recent**: streams with unread messages or activity, and any stream whose last message is under seven days old.
- **Everything Else**: the rest. It starts collapsed.

Muted streams don't move up on unread messages.

Next to a stream's name, an unread marker is coloured by what is waiting:

- Red: someone mentioned you.
- Amber: the latest message is from an AI persona.
- Green: the latest message is from a bot.
- Blue: other unread messages or activity.

Threads appear nested under their parent stream's row when the parent is in the sidebar. Each stream row has a menu with its settings, labels, files and more.

## Getting started checklist

Above the user menu, a **Getting started** card shows a progress count while some tasks are still open. Its items are:

- **Turn on notifications**, shown only when your browser supports push and the server has it enabled.
- **Add a profile photo**, which opens your profile settings.
- **Write your first note**, which creates a scratchpad.
- **Meet Ariadne**, which opens a scratchpad where Ariadne greets you and shows you around (see [Meet Ariadne](/guide/meet-ariadne)).
- **Invite your team**, shown to workspace owners and admins only.

Each item ticks off by itself once it is true. Dismiss the card with its X button. If tasks remain, **Getting started** in the user menu brings it back.

## User menu

The menu at the bottom of the sidebar, opened from your avatar, contains:

- **Pause notifications** or **Resume notifications**.
- **Getting started**, when the checklist can be restored.
- **Customize sidebar**.
- **Appearance**, with System, Light and Dark themes.
- **Settings** and **Workspace Settings**.
- **AI Usage**.
- **App status**.
- **Switch account** and **Log out**. See [Signing in and switching accounts](/guide/sign-in-and-accounts).

On a touch device the same menu opens as a drawer titled "Account menu".

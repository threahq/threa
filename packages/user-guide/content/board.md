# The board

The [board](app:board) is one feed of the conversations from all your streams, with the most recent activity first. Each conversation is a card with its recent messages and a reply box, so you can catch up and answer without opening each channel. For how conversations are formed, see [Conversations and topics](/guide/conversations-and-topics).

Open it from the **Board** link in the sidebar, or run **View Board** from the [quick switcher](/guide/quick-switcher-and-command-palette). While you are on the board, the sidebar switches to a board mode with the filters, views and lenses described below. Its **Chats** link takes you back to your last stream.

## The feed

Cards are grouped under Today, Yesterday, Earlier this week, This month and Older. A card has these controls:

- **Expand conversation** and **Collapse conversation** change how much of the card is shown.
- **Open conversation** opens the whole conversation in the side panel.
- The **Conversation actions** menu has **Rename topic…** (a scratchpad's own conversation shows **Rename scratchpad…**), **Regenerate title** when the title was set by hand or predates automatic titles, **Mark resolved** or **Reopen**, **Copy link**, **Split with AI…**, **Open an aside** where the stream allows one, and **Hide from board**. Split is not offered for scratchpads.
- The reply box ("Write a reply…") files your message into that conversation.

When new activity arrives while you are reading, a floating pill such as "3 updates" appears at the top. Click it to bring the new activity in. The round **New post** button at the bottom right opens a composer where you pick the stream to post in, or start a new scratchpad.

## Lenses

A lens is a base view. Open one from the **Lenses** list in the board sidebar. Its choice is kept in the page address as `board?lens=`.

- **All** shows everything, newest activity first.
- **Mine** shows conversations you are in or mentioned in.

## Filters

The **Filters** group in the board sidebar narrows the feed. Each picker is a list where a row can be included or excluded with its ban icon. Excluded items appear as "Not:" chips.

- **Streams** picks individual channels, DMs and scratchpads, with a search box. Each list holds up to 50 streams.
- **Type** picks Channels, DMs, Scratchpads or System.
- **Labels** picks from your own [labels](/guide/labels). It appears once you have a label.
- **Unread** shows only conversations in streams that have unread messages for you. Streams you have muted are left out. A card you have read stays in the view until you leave it or press **Clear from unread** on the card.
- **Archived** adds conversations from archived streams, which are hidden by default.
- **With drafts** shows only conversations where you have a draft.

The bell icon on a row in the Streams picker mutes that stream from the board. A muted stream stays off the board unless you name it in the Streams filter. This is separate from [notification levels](/guide/notification-levels).

Active filters are listed under **Filtering the board**, with **Clear** to remove them all and **Save view** to keep them.

## Saved views

**Save view** asks for a name ("View name") and stores the lens together with the stream, type and label filters, included and excluded. Unread, Archived and With drafts are not stored. Saved views are listed under **Views** in the board sidebar, and a view is highlighted while the current filters match it. Each view has an actions menu with **Set as board home**, **Rename** and **Delete**.

## Hiding a conversation

**Hide from board** in a conversation's menu removes the card from your board only. The conversation comes back by itself when it gets new activity. To bring it back sooner, open it from search, Saved or a link and choose **Unhide from board**.

## Board home

Opening the board with no lens or view lands on your board home, which starts as the **All** lens. Change it by choosing **Set as board home** from a lens or view menu, or in the **Board home** section of your [Appearance settings](app:settings/appearance). Either a lens or one of your saved views can be the home. If the saved view is deleted, the board falls back to the lens.

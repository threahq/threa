# Dates, time zones and working hours

The **Date & Time** and **Working hours** tabs of your [settings](app:settings/datetime) control how times are written and when your working day starts.

## Date & Time

- **Date Format**: **2025-01-15 (ISO)** (the default), **15/01/2025 (European)** or **01/15/2025 (US)**.
- **Time Format**: **14:30 (24-hour)** (the default) or **2:30 PM (12-hour)**.
- **Home Timezone**: shown to colleagues and used for time-aware features. Times in the app are shown in your device's local timezone, whatever this is set to. The picker also shows the timezone your device is set to. The default is UTC.

## Working hours

The [Working hours](app:settings/schedule) tab sets your working week and hours. Scheduling shortcuts such as "Tomorrow morning" and "Next week" snap to when you start work, in your local time.

By default you inherit the workspace schedule, which workspace admins set (see [Workspace statuses and schedule](/guide/workspace-statuses-and-schedule)). Turn on **Use a custom schedule (otherwise inherit the workspace default)** to set your own:

- Each weekday starts from Monday and has a switch. A day that is off shows **Day off**.
- Each day has shifts with a **Shift start** and a **Shift end**. **Add shift** adds another, up to 6 per day, and the X removes one.
- **Reset** undoes your edits and **Save schedule** saves them.

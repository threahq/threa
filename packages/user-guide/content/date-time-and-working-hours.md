# Dates, time zones and working hours

The [Time & schedule](app:settings/datetime) page of your settings controls how times are written and when your working day starts.

## Time zone & format

- **Time zone**: shown to others and used by agents and reminders. Times in the app are shown in your device's local timezone, whatever this is set to. When your device is on a different zone, the page says so and offers **Use this device's time zone**. The default is UTC.
- **Clock**: **14:30** (the default) or **2:30 PM**.
- **Date format**: **2026-01-31** (year first, the default), **31/01/2026** (day first) or **01/31/2026** (month first).

## Working hours

**Your working hours** sets your working week and hours. Scheduling shortcuts such as "Tomorrow morning" and "Next week" snap to when you start work, in your local time.

By default you use the workspace schedule, which workspace admins set (see [Workspace statuses and schedule](/guide/workspace-statuses-and-schedule)). Open the row and choose **Customize** to set your own, or **Use the workspace's hours** to go back:

- Each weekday starts from Monday and has a switch. A day that is off shows **Day off**.
- Each day has shifts with a start and an end time. **Add shift** adds another, up to 6 per day, and the X removes one.
- Each edit saves right away.

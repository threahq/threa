# Quoting and sharing messages

Quote puts a message in your composer so your reply sits next to what it answers. Share inserts a message from one stream into another. Both stay tied to the original message.

## Quote a message

- Hover a message and click **Quote reply**, or choose **Quote reply** from its menu.
- On a touch screen, swipe a message to the left.
- To quote only part of a message, select the text with a mouse. A floating **Quote** button appears next to the selection.

The quote shows up in your composer as a card with the author and the quoted text. Write your reply under it and send. To drop the quote, use its **Remove quote** button. A quote pins the version of the message that was on screen, and the part you selected if you selected one.

On a touch screen, long-press the message to open its actions drawer. Tap the message preview at the top to select a passage, and a floating **Quote** and **Share** pill appears next to the selection. The drawer is covered in [Every message action](/guide/message-actions-reference).

## Reply in conversation

When a message belongs to a conversation, **Reply in conversation** in its menu files your next message into that conversation. It is not shown in encrypted streams. See [Conversations and topics](/guide/conversations-and-topics).

## Share a message

**Share message** opens a picker titled **Share message**. Search for a stream (a toggle changes the sort order), pick it, and Threa opens that stream with the message already in the composer. Add a comment if you want and send. The picker lists channels, direct messages and your scratchpads, and skips archived streams.

Select text with the mouse and click **Share** next to **Quote** to share only that part. The picker previews exactly what will be shared.

Inside a thread, the message menu also has a one-click entry for the stream the thread belongs to, labeled **Share to #channel-name**, **Share to DM** or **Share to scratchpad**. In a thread nested inside another thread, there is one more entry for the parent thread, labeled **Share to thread (name)**.

The shared message appears as a card. If the original was edited after you shared it, the card keeps showing the shared version and adds an **Edited since** link. If the original was deleted, the card reads "Message deleted by author".

If a share would expose the source to people outside its stream, Threa warns "This share would expose the source to people outside the source stream." and offers **Share anyway** or **Cancel**.

### Encrypted messages

Sharing a message from an [encrypted scratchpad](/guide/encrypted-scratchpads) asks "Share this encrypted message?". Choosing **Share & decrypt** posts the contents as a normal message that everyone in the destination can read. You can still quote an encrypted message, but you can't share only a part of it.

## Copy a link to a message

**Copy link to message** puts a link to the message on your clipboard. Opening the link takes the reader to that message in its stream, provided they have access to the stream.

Sharing from your phone's other apps into Threa is covered in [Share to Threa](/guide/share-to-threa).

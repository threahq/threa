# Sharing into Threa from your phone

Threa registers itself as a share target, so on systems that support it, Threa appears in the share menu once it is installed. Sharing a page, a piece of text or a file to Threa opens a picker where you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent until you press send in the composer. See [Installing Threa as an app](/guide/install-the-app).

## What you can share

- Text, a link and a title. When the title differs from the text, it becomes a bold first line, followed by the text and then the link.
- Files: images, video, audio, PDFs and text files.

## The Share to Threa picker

After you pick Threa in the share menu, Threa opens **Share to Threa**. It shows a preview of what you shared and a list of places to put it:

- The **New scratchpad** item is always first. It creates a scratchpad with the shared content in its composer, named after the shared title if there is one.
- Your existing scratchpads, channels and direct messages follow. Archived streams are not listed.
- Type in **Search streams...** to filter the list. The two buttons beside it sort by recent activity or A to Z.

Choose a place and Threa opens it with the shared content as a draft in the composer. Files are uploaded and attached to the draft. If an upload fails, the text is still saved and that file is left out. If that place already has a draft, the shared text replaces the draft's text and the files are added to its attachments. Add a note if you like, then send.

## Things to know

- Sharing always goes to the first workspace in your workspace list.
- If you are signed out, Threa asks you to sign in first.
- If Threa cannot tell which signed-in account a share was meant for, it shows "The shared content was not saved" and asks you to share again while online.
- Shares are tied to the account that received them. If you switch accounts before choosing a place, the content stays with the original account and is not placed in the new one.

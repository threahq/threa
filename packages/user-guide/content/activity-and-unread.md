# Activity and unread

The [Activity](app:activity) page lists what happened that involves you. Unread markers inside a stream show where you left off.

## The Activity page

Activity has three tabs:

- **All**: activity from other people. When some of it is unread, it is split into **Unread** and **Earlier** sections.
- **Unread**: only unread items.
- **Me**: rows from your own actions. These are already read.

Items include mentions, messages, reactions, reminders, new memberships and missed calls. Click an unread row to mark it read and go to the message, the conversation panel or, for a standalone reminder, the Saved page (see [Saved and reminders](/guide/saved-and-reminders)).

**Mark all read** appears at the top when you have unread items. The empty page says **No activity yet** and explains that mentions will show up here. The **Unread** tab says **You're all caught up!** when nothing is left.

The feed records activity even while notifications are paused. Pausing holds back only push (see [Pausing notifications and setting a status](/guide/pause-and-status)).

## Unread inside a stream

A line labelled **New** marks where your unread messages begin. Where a stream opens is your choice, under **Opening unread streams** in your [appearance settings](app:settings/appearance):

- **At the latest message** (the default): the stream opens at the bottom, and an **N new messages** button jumps up to where you left off.
- **At the first unread**: the stream opens where you left off, and a **Jump to latest** button takes you to the bottom.

## Mark as read and unread

In a message's menu:

- **Mark as read** marks everything up to that message as read. It appears on unread messages.
- **Mark as unread** makes that message and everything after it unread. It appears on read messages.

See [Message actions reference](/guide/message-actions-reference).

## Settling

The **Settling** setting in the same place chooses which actions remove a stream from the Inbox: **When you reply or react** (the default), **Only when you settle it** or **When you read it**. See [Appearance and accessibility](/guide/appearance-and-accessibility).

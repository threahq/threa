# Composer and keyboard settings

The [Keyboard & composer](app:settings/keyboard) page of your settings holds how sending and attaching work, and the list of keyboard shortcuts. For the shortcuts themselves see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Sending

**Enter key** chooses what Enter does:

- **Send message**: the default. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- **Start a new line**: Ctrl+Enter (⌘Enter on Mac) sends.

**Send button side** puts the composer's send button on the **Right** (the default) or the **Left**.

## Composer

On mobile, **Insert files at the cursor** adds a picked file as a chip inside the message. It is on by default, and removing the chip keeps the file attached. See [Attachments and files](/guide/attachments-and-files).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts are listed in three groups: **Navigation shortcuts**, **View shortcuts** and **Editing shortcuts**. The **Filter by name or keys** box narrows the list, and **Changed** shows only the ones you have changed. To change one:

1. Click its key badge. The badge reads **Press keys…**.
2. Press the new combination. Press Esc to cancel.

A shortcut needs the Ctrl key (⌘ on Mac), the Alt key (⌥ on Mac), or an F1 to F12 key. A bare letter key can't be bound, and Esc is reserved. App shortcuts take priority over editing shortcuts when they clash.

If the combination already belongs to another action, the row says which one and offers **Move to** the new action, or **Keep for** the current owner. Shortcuts that share keys are marked with "Same keys as" and the other action.

A row you have changed shows a reset button (**Reset to default**). **Reset to defaults** at the top of the shortcuts restores every one and appears once you have changed any. A shortcut with no key shows **Not set**.

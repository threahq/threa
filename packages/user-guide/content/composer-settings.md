# Composer and keyboard settings

The **Composer** tab of your [settings](app:settings/keyboard) holds how sending and attaching work, and the list of keyboard shortcuts. For the shortcuts themselves see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Send Messages

Choose what sends a message:

- **Enter to send**: the default.
- **⌘/Ctrl + Enter to send**.

## File attachments

On mobile, **Insert files at the cursor** adds a picked file as a chip inside the message. It is on by default, and removing the chip keeps the file attached. See [Attachments and files](/guide/attachments-and-files).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts are listed in three groups: **Navigation**, **View** and **Editing**. To change one:

1. Click its key badge. The badge reads **Press keys...** and a popover says **Press shortcut keys**.
2. Press the new combination. Press Esc to cancel.

A shortcut needs the Ctrl key (⌘ on Mac), the Alt key (⌥ on Mac), or an F1 to F12 key. A bare letter key can't be bound, and Esc is reserved. App shortcuts take priority over editing shortcuts when they clash.

If the combination already belongs to another action, a **Move shortcut?** dialog asks what to do. Choose **Move to** the new action, or keep the current owner. A **Shortcut Conflicts** section at the top of the tab appears whenever two shortcuts clash.

A row you have changed shows a **Reset to default** button. **Reset all shortcuts** restores every one and appears once you have changed any. A shortcut with no key shows a dash.

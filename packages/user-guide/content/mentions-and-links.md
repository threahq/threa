# Mentions and channel links

Three characters open a suggestion list in the composer: **@** for mentions, **#** for links to streams and **:** for emoji. Keep typing to narrow the list, and use the arrow keys and Enter to pick an entry.

## Mentions with @

Type **@** and a name. The list matches against both the handle and the display name. It can contain:

- Workspace members, labelled **User**. Your own entry comes first and is marked "(me)".
- Ariadne and other AI personas, labelled **AI**.
- Bots that are members of the current stream, labelled **Bot**.
- **Channel** and **Here**, labelled **Notify**.

A personal bot owned by someone else shows **Mention only** with the note "only its owner can invoke it". Bots that are not members of the stream do not appear.

**@channel** appears in channels and in threads under a channel. It notifies every member of the channel. **@here** appears in channels, direct messages and threads under either. It notifies the members of the stream you are writing in. In scratchpads neither is offered. The people mentioned are notified according to their [notification settings](/guide/notification-levels).

Mentions in a message are stored as links to the person or agent itself. A mention of someone whose handle Threa does not recognise stays as plain text.

## Channel links with the # sign

Type **#** and part of a name to link to a channel or a scratchpad. Channels are listed before scratchpads. Archived streams and streams without a name are not offered. Type **##** to list channels only.

## Emoji with :

Type **:** followed by a few letters of an emoji name to open the emoji picker. The grid shows a **Recently used** section above the full list. Picking an entry inserts the emoji and a space. The picker does not open inside code blocks or inline code. For reacting to a message instead, see [Emoji and reactions](/guide/emoji-and-reactions).

The composer's action bar has **Mention** and **Emoji** buttons that open the @ and : lists. There is no button for #. See [Writing and sending messages](/guide/composer-basics).

# Every message action

Each message has a menu of actions. Which ones you see depends on who wrote the message, where it is shown and whether the stream is encrypted, so the menu is shorter on some messages than others.

## Reach the menu

On desktop, hover a message. A toolbar appears with an emoji picker, **Save for later**, **Quote reply**, **Reply in thread** and **Message actions** (the three-dot button), which opens the full menu.

On a touch screen, press and hold a message to open a bottom sheet. It shows a preview of the message (tap it to highlight a passage), a row of reaction emoji, and the list of actions. Swipe a message to the left to quote it. Keep the finger down and drag it down to open an [aside](/guide/asides) on the message, where one can be opened.

## Reply and share

- **Reply in thread** opens the message's [thread](/guide/threads). The menu entry is hidden on the message a thread panel is already open on; the toolbar button stays.
- **Quote reply** puts the message in the composer as a quote.
- **Reply in conversation** files your next message into the message's [conversation](/guide/conversations-and-topics). It is not offered in encrypted streams.
- **Show in conversation** opens that conversation in the side panel.
- **Share message** opens a picker to share the message into another stream. In a thread, a one-click entry such as **Share to #channel-name**, **Share to DM** or **Share to scratchpad** sends it to the stream the thread belongs to. A nested thread also gets an entry for its parent thread, such as **Share to thread (name)**.

See [Quoting and sharing messages](/guide/quoting-and-sharing).

## Save and organize

- **Save for later** saves the message. Once it is saved, the menu shows **Remove from Saved** instead.
- **Set reminder…** saves the message with a reminder.
- **Label message** files it under one of your [labels](/guide/labels).

See [Saved messages, to-dos and reminders](/guide/saved-and-reminders).

## Your own messages

- **Edit message** appears on your own messages, except in encrypted streams.
- **See revisions** appears on any message that has been edited.
- **Delete message** appears on your own messages.

See [Editing, deleting and revisions](/guide/editing-and-revisions).

## Move

- **Move to thread…** starts a move with this message selected. It is not shown on a thread's parent message, in an archived stream or while you are already selecting messages.
- **Show move details** appears on a message that arrived in the stream through a move.
- **Move to conversation…** and **Move messages to conversation…** appear while the conversation overlay is on. They re-file one message, or several selected ones, into another conversation.
- **Keep here** and **Not this topic…** appear on a message whose conversation placement is still settling, in the conversation panel and on board cards. **Keep here** confirms it. **Not this topic…** opens the picker to pick another conversation.
- **New sub-topic** and **Move to sub-topic…** appear in the conversation panel and on board cards. **New sub-topic** shows only on a message with no thread yet.

See [Moving messages](/guide/moving-messages) and [Conversations and topics](/guide/conversations-and-topics).

## Messages outside their stream

On board cards, in the conversation panel and on a label's page, a message also has a **View in channel** entry (the noun follows the stream type: thread, DM or scratchpad) that opens the message in its own stream.

## Agent messages

- **Show trace and sources** appears on a message from an agent that was sent during a session. It opens the trace for that session.
- **Insert into draft** appears on an agent's messages inside an aside. It copies the message into your composer as a block that names its author. It is not offered in encrypted streams.
- **Open an aside here** starts an aside anchored to the message. It is available in channels, DMs, scratchpads and threads, but not in encrypted or archived streams.

## Read state, copy and links

- **Mark as read** marks everything up to the message as read. It is hidden on a message that is already read.
- **Mark as unread** makes the message and everything after it unread. It is hidden on a message that is already unread.
- **Copy as Markdown** and **Copy as Plain text** copy the message text.
- **Copy link to message** copies a link that opens the stream at that message.

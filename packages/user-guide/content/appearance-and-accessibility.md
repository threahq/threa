# Appearance and accessibility

How Threa looks is on the [Appearance](app:settings/appearance) page of your settings. How messages, the Inbox and the board behave is on the [Reading](app:settings/messages) and [Sidebar & board](app:settings/inbox) pages. Number fields take effect when you leave the field or press Enter, and values outside the allowed range are adjusted to fit.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system**. The default is Match system.
- **Message density**: **Comfortable** or **Compact**. The default is Comfortable.
- **Text size**: **Small** (14 px), **Medium** (16 px, the default) or **Large** (18 px).
- **Font**: **Default**, **Monospace** or **OpenDyslexic**.
- **Reduce motion**: turns off animations and transitions. Off by default.
- **High contrast**: stronger secondary text, borders, controls and focus rings. Off by default.

## Reading

- **When there are unreads**: **At the latest message** (the default; an **N new messages** button jumps up to where you left off) or **At your first unread** (a **Jump to latest** button takes you back down).
- **Long messages**: **Never** (the default) or **Taller than** a height. **Collapse messages taller than** (default 420 px, range 120 to 4000) is how tall a message must be to start collapsed behind Show more. **Collapsed message height** (default 240 px, range 80 to 2000) is how much stays visible.
- **Code blocks**: **Collapse long code blocks** is **Never**, **Always** or **Taller than** a number of lines (default 10, up to 500). **Long lines**: **Scroll** (the default) or **Wrap**. **Per language** sets an override for one language with **Add a language**.
- **Quotes**: **Collapse long quotes** works the same way (default 6 lines, up to 500).

## Sidebar & board

- **Remove from Inbox**: which actions remove a stream from the Inbox. **When you reply or react** is the default, then **Only when you settle it** and **As soon as you read it**.
- **When you drag a labeled stream**: **Ask each time** (the default), **Remove the old label** or **Keep the old label**. See [Labels](/guide/labels).
- **Default view**: where Board opens, a lens or a saved view (see [Board](/guide/board)).
- **Collapse tall cards**: **Never** (the default) or **Taller than** a height. **Collapse cards taller than** (default 600 px, range 120 to 4000) is how tall a card must be to start collapsed. **Collapsed card height** (default 320 px, range 120 to 2000) is how much stays visible.
- **Older messages on cards**: **Newest messages in full** (default 1, range 0 to 10), **One-line messages per card** (default 15, range 5 to 100), **One-line message length** (default 110 characters, range 40 to 300) and **Badge on collapsed messages**: **Unread count** (the default) or **None**.

The send button's side is on **Keyboard & composer**. See [Composer and keyboard settings](/guide/composer-settings).

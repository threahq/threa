# Appearance and accessibility

Open the **Appearance** and **Accessibility** tabs of your [settings](app:settings/appearance). Number fields take effect when you leave the field or press Enter, and values outside the allowed range are adjusted to fit.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. The default is System.
- **Message Display**: **Compact** or **Comfortable**. The default is Comfortable.
- **Opening unread streams**: **At the latest message** (the default; an **N new messages** button jumps up to where you left off) or **At the first unread** (a **Jump to latest** button takes you back down).
- **Settling**: which actions remove a stream from the Inbox. **When you reply or react** is the default, then **Only when you settle it** and **When you read it**.
- **Code Blocks**: the collapse threshold in lines (default 10, range 0 to 500) and **Long lines**: **Scroll horizontally** (the default) or **Wrap lines**. **Add language** sets an override for one language.
- **Block Quotes**: the collapse threshold (default 6, range 0 to 500).
- **Long Messages**: **Collapse long messages automatically** is off by default. **Collapse at height** (default 420 px, range 120 to 4000) is how tall a message must be to get Show more/less, and when automatic collapsing applies. **Collapse to height** (default 240 px, range 80 to 2000) is how much stays visible.
- **Board Cards**: **Collapse tall board cards automatically** is off by default. **Collapse at height** (default 600 px, range 120 to 4000) is how tall a card must be to start collapsed when this is on. **Collapse to height** (default 320 px, range 120 to 2000) is how much stays visible.
- **Board Ledger**: **Messages shown in full** (default 1, range 0 to 10), **Ledger rows** (default 15, range 5 to 100), **Lead line length** (default 110, range 40 to 300) and **Head row badge**: **Unread message count** (the default) or **No badge**.
- **Board home**: choose a lens or a saved view (see [Board](/guide/board)).
- **Moving labeled streams**: **Ask each time** (the default), **Remove the old label** or **Keep the old label**. See [Labels](/guide/labels).

## Accessibility

- **Reduce motion**: off by default.
- **High contrast**: off by default.
- **Composer actions**: place the composer's action buttons on the **Right** (the default) or the **Left**.
- **Font Size**: **Small** (14 px), **Medium** (16 px, the default) or **Large** (18 px).
- **Font Family**: **Default**, **Monospace** or **OpenDyslexic**.

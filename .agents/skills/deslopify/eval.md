# Deslopify self-check

Run this after every rewrite. Fix the draft if any applicable check fails.

For detect mode, skip the rewrite checks. Confirm that every finding names a
pattern, quotes the affected text, and gives a short fix without rewriting or
guessing whether AI wrote it.

## Meaning and voice

- Does the edit preserve the writer's point without adding claims, examples,
  numbers, opinions, quotations, or sources?
- Does it retain distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty,
  rough edges, and useful digressions?
- Were clean, human sentences left alone rather than normalized for consistency?
- Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual problem?
- Are technical terms, identifiers, behavior, and concrete details unchanged?

## Clarity

- Does the copy lead with the point when the setup adds nothing?
- Are actors and actions explicit where passive phrasing or false agency hid them?
- Are tangled sentences clearer without flattening deliberate cadence?
- Did direct verbs replace weak verb phrases where useful?
- Were vague abstractions replaced only when the draft supplied a concrete fact?

## Slop patterns

- Are rhetorical contrasts, triples, negative lists, rhetorical questions, and
  dramatic fragments gone unless technically necessary?
- Are throat-clearing, faux-insight setups, dramatic colon reveals, decorative
  analysis, importance puffery, vague attribution, and invented labels gone?
- Are precise terms repeated instead of synonym-cycled?
- Is the rhythm natural rather than mechanically symmetrical?
- Are fake-profound and recap endings removed?
- Are clichés, filler, hype, unsupported aspirations, and competitor callouts gone?
- Is formatting structural rather than decorative: no formulaic accent words,
  emoji headings, repeated bold-first bullets, or headings over tiny sections?
- Are there no em dashes in edited copy?

## Final read

- Would the writer recognize this as their own voice?
- Would it sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
- For pasted text, does the response include the edited copy and a short
  material-change note? For in-repo edits, does it name only the files and
  material changes?

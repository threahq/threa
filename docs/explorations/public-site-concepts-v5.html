Iteration · v5 · 2026-05-24

Tightening Filmstrip, clarifying the needle's flow, and pulling typography off the AI-startup default.

Three shifts: (1) the dark band is sized to the content, no empty headroom above the headline; (2) the pass-through is visually explicit — ribbon enters left, threads through the vesica, exits right, with a brighter, "kept" segment on the way out; (3) typography moves off the Newsreader-italic-accent default. Each concept commits to a different pairing so we can read which voice fits Threa best.

Copy posture No comparisons to other tools on the home page. The pitch is what Threa does — conversations leave a trace, decisions surface when they matter, the half-formed has a place to live. The mechanism (AI) shows up later in the page or not at all on the front door.

Direction

Filmstrip, slimmed down to a band sized to the content — no decorative headroom. Copy on the left fills the band, brand mark plus a small annotated key on the right. The "ribbon" is a single luminous edge at the bottom of the band: less SVG curve, more like a line of stage light. Plex Sans throughout reads engineered without tipping into "AI-startup serif italic".

Display + body: IBM Plex Sans · Meta: IBM Plex Mono
Where it sings
  • No wasted vertical space — reads immediately
  • Sans-only restores "made by engineers" feel
  • The annotated key earns its place by being legible
Where it hesitates
  • Without serif, hero risks reading utilitarian / cold
  • Annotated key competes with the headline for attention
  • Plex Sans is a strong choice but used by IBM — could feel borrowed

Direction

The dark element is rotated vertical — a tall pillar on the right of the hero, holding the brand mark with a ribbon threading top-to-bottom through it. Copy occupies the cream side, breathing wide. The pillar's footer shows a single “held” line that proves the metaphor without committing to a screenshot. Source Serif 4 is the academic-considered headline; Plus Jakarta keeps the body warm.

Display: Source Serif 4 · Body: Plus Jakarta Sans · Meta: IBM Plex Mono
Where it sings
  • Vertical pillar reads as a "thing on display", not a stripe
  • Lots of breathing room for the headline without giant padding
  • Source Serif 4 is genuinely less common than Newsreader / Fraunces
Where it hesitates
  • Vertical layout fights mobile harder than horizontal
  • Pillar can read as a card / phone mockup if not styled carefully
  • Two distinct columns split the eye — needs a strong copy/visual relationship

Where to from here

Five tighter blends. Tell me which voice (typography) lands and which threading layout reads cleanest — or mix-and-match across concepts (e.g. "01's tightness with 03's flow"). Once one wins, I'll adapt it into About (longer-form why, contact form) and Developer (API reference, examples, recipes), then port the static doc into a real framework with live tokens from the product.

Worth deciding while looking:

  • All-sans (01) vs. serif headline (02, 03, 04, 05)?
  • Concrete artefact (02) vs. abstract metaphor (01, 03, 04, 05)?
  • Horizontal band (01, 02, 04) vs. centered stage (03) vs. vertical pillar (05)?
  • Should the threading have a "before / after" reading like 03 explicitly invites?

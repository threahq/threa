Iteration · v3 · 2026-05-23

Five new directions that hold the cleanliness of Stage Light with the woven pulse of The Loom.

You liked Stage Light's restraint and The Loom's vibe. These five push that combination from different angles — where the kinetic gold lives, how dark or light the room is, how the page breathes. The product vocabulary is generic across the board: chat, notes, memory, a quiet AI companion. The in-house terms can come back in product surfaces and onboarding, not on the front door.

From Stage Light Centered focal moment. Single confident claim. Generous whitespace. Editorial display type for the hero. The cream paper backdrop and one clean CTA.
From The Loom A living gold element somewhere in the frame — thread, ring, ribbon, weave. Slow motion, never busy. The sense that something is happening underneath the page, quietly.

THREA
STOCKHOLM · 2026

Direction

A horizontal gold ribbon flows through the page — the hero, the row of value props, the product moment, the CTA — like a single thought running across the whole front door. Newsreader italic carries the headline; everything else gives the ribbon room to breathe. The kinetic element is the dashed line travelling along the ribbon, gentle and continuous.

Where it sings
  • Most literal expression of "thread connects everything"
  • Light page + continuous motion = lively, not loud
  • Product moment grounds the abstract metaphor
Where it hesitates
  • Ribbon needs careful layout on mobile (likely vertical there)
  • Two SVG curves can clutter if not tuned to copy positions
  • The "AI replies to you" moment leans product-demo; About page will need a quieter angle

Where to from here

Reactions welcome — on any one of the five, or on what's still missing. Once one direction lands, I'll adapt it into About (longer-form why, contact form) and Developer (API reference, examples, recipes). The static doc can then be ported into Astro or Vite, with tokens pulled live from apps/frontend/src/index.css so the marketing site can't drift from the product palette.

Specific things worth deciding while looking:

  • Dark hero (01, 03) vs all-light (02, 04, 05)?
  • Is the headline a single confident claim, or three rhythmic beats?
  • Do we want a product moment on the home page (01, 05), or save it for Product/About?
  • How strong should the AI / memory beat be on the front door — lead, equal, or quiet?
